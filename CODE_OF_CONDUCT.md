# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue with the work, not the person. Assume the other reader is
competent and acting in good faith until shown otherwise.

## Expected behaviour

* **Critique claims, not people.** "This experiment does not support that
  conclusion because X" is welcome and useful. "You clearly do not understand
  security" is neither.
* **Show your reasoning.** This is a research repository: assertions without a
  reproduction, a test, or a citation are opinions, and are treated as such.
* **Accept correction.** Several of the design decisions in this project exist
  because an experiment disproved the previous one. That is the normal case.
* **Respect scope.** Offensive tooling is out of scope (see
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)); proposals for it will be closed.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation,
sustained disruption, publishing others' private information, or sexualised
language or imagery in any project space.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any space
where someone is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn, edit or remove contributions, or
block accounts, proportionate to the behaviour. Maintainers who do not follow
this document are subject to it as well — the project's whole thesis is that the
enforcing party is also governed.

Inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
rewritten for a research repository.
